Simple Client Follow-Up System

If client follow-up keeps slipping, a checklist will fix more of it than a new tool will. Here's the short version — steal it, cut what doesn't fit, keep the rest.

Before you start

  • Decide what "done" looks like. One sentence.
  • Decide who owns it. One name.
  • Decide when it gets checked. One day of the week.

While it's running

  • Log the date, the owner, the outcome. Three columns, that's it.
  • If something takes more than two attempts, that's a process problem, not a people problem.
  • If it's been skipped twice, either automate it or kill it.

After it runs

  • Compare to the last time. Any drift?
  • One thing to change. Not three.
  • Write down why you changed it. Future you will not remember.
