Weekly Business Dashboard Setup

Setting up business dashboard from scratch takes an afternoon if you keep it small. Most people spend a week because they try to solve every edge case on day one.

Step 1 — Draft the smallest version

One file, one list, one owner. If it can't be done in an hour, it's too big.

Step 2 — Run it for a week manually

No automation. Just run the steps by hand and watch where they hurt. The pain points tell you what to build next.

Step 3 — Fix the biggest pain point

One change. Then run it another week. Resist the urge to do two.

Step 4 — Automate only what's proven

By week three you'll know which step is worth scripting and which one is fine as-is. Automate the proven one.
