Small Business Cash Flow Checklist

The same four mistakes show up in almost every small business cash flow setup. None of them are exotic. All of them cost real hours.

1. Buying a tool before defining the process

Software is a multiplier. Multiply a bad process and you get a faster bad process. Write the steps first, then decide whether anything needs to be bought.

2. Keeping two sources of truth

A spreadsheet and a tool. A doc and an inbox. Whichever one gets updated less becomes wrong first, and nobody notices until it matters.

3. Automating the wrong step

People automate the part they find annoying, not the part that takes the longest. Time the steps. Automate the slow one.

4. No review loop

If nothing ever looks at the output, quality drifts. A five-minute weekly check catches more than any dashboard.
