Productivity Stack for Solo Businesses

A monthly review is the cheapest upgrade you can make to productivity. Twenty minutes, once a month, and the drift stops compounding.

What to look at

  • Volume — how much moved through, compared to last month.
  • Time — how long the slowest step took.
  • Breakage — what went wrong, and whether it was the same thing as last month.

What to write down

One number per item, and one sentence of context. Anything longer doesn't get read next month.

What to change

One thing. The review's job is to pick the next single change, not to fix everything at once.

Bottom line

Reviews are how a productivity setup stays honest. Without them, the process drifts and nobody can say when it started.
